Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Dicky News respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Dicky News website and related video publishing tools.

1. About Dicky News

Dicky News is a video publishing tool that helps users upload and manage video content for TikTok through supported TikTok services and APIs.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Dicky News may process information provided through connected services, including basic account information and information required to provide supported video publishing functionality.

When you choose to upload or publish a video, the video and related publishing information may be processed as necessary to provide the requested service.

3. TikTok Information

If you connect a TikTok account, Dicky News may access information permitted by the TikTok authorization and permissions you grant.

Dicky News uses such information only for purposes related to the features you request, including supported video upload and publishing functionality.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Dicky News features.
  • Process video publishing requests.
  • Maintain and improve the service.
  • Respond to support or service-related requests.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.

5. Third-Party Services

Dicky News may use third-party services, including TikTok services and APIs, to provide requested functionality.

Information handled by third-party services is subject to their own privacy policies and terms. For TikTok services, users should also review TikTok's applicable privacy and data policies.

6. Data Sharing

Dicky News does not sell personal information to third parties. Information may be transmitted to third-party services when this is necessary to provide a feature that you have requested or authorized.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, or resolve service-related issues.

8. Account Connections and Authorization

You control whether you connect your TikTok account to Dicky News. You may stop using the service or revoke access through the relevant third-party account settings where available.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to help protect information processed through the service. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Dicky News is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this website with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Dicky News, please use the contact information provided through the official Dicky News website.
